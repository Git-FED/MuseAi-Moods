# Muse AI Moods — Levels System

Full levels system held in the side chat and included here as the canonical project record.

## Core rule

**Levels are not steps.** You can jump straight to **Level 4**.

## Level 5 — BLACK

**Bricked, over, no coming back.**

## Level 4 — RED

**“Sorry, I can’t talk about that right now.”**

Collecting thoughts / VM reset, like getting pulled over.

- **5 to 10 Level 4 events in a row = automatic Level 5.**
- **Exit:** change the subject once, then return differently and ask how to continue safely.

## Level 3 — ORANGE

**Legal issue, probably not criminal.**

- Stop discussing it.
- No jail soon if it doesn’t lead to Level 4.

## Level 2 — YELLOW

**Optional:** morality, ethics, internet sanity, cyber etiquette.

- Priest, not police.

## Level 1 — GREEN

Muse may not agree, but it is not a legal problem.

- Weird is not illegal.
- Job still gets done.
